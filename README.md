# Spotify for Emerging Talent

## 📌 Executive Summary
The Spotify ecosystem is currently highly concentrated. Well-established artists dominate the platform's algorithms, creating severe entry barriers for emerging talent. This project identifies critical algorithmic thresholds, evaluates the strategic shift toward single releases, and outlines the long-term risks to platform stability.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. The Mainstream Threshold
* Data reveals a strict empirical probability barrier for breaking into the mainstream catalog.
* **The Threshold:** An artist whose prior track peaked between a popularity rank of 20 and 58 has a narrow **13% empirical probability** of breaking through the rank 58 threshold into mainstream.

### 2. Algorithmic Dominance
* The top 15 established global artists command **30% share** of all high-ranking tracks (ranked between 70 to 100).
* Current recommendation algorithms heavily favor already-established artists (e.g., Taylor Swift, Bad Bunny, The Weeknd), limiting organic discovery for new talent.

### 3. Strategic Release of Singles
* The trend of releasing singles has grown exponentially with no signs of slowing down.
* In 2025 alone, **1.37 times** more tracks were released as singles compared to full albums.
* **The Loophole:** Spotify’s algorithm rewards high-frequency, consistent content. Releasing standalone singles yields higher track ranking for emerging artists compared to albums.

---

## ⚠️ Platform Implications & Risks

* **Catalog Stagnation:** Relying on a small pool of mega-hits leads to content stagnation and listener fatigue.
* **Loss of Trust:** Independent creators realize the platform's infrastructure is stacked against them, risking a migration of talent to alternative distribution channels.
* **Loss of Leverage:** Continued dependence on a highly concentrated pool of royalty users reduces Spotify's bargaining leverage during major record label negotiations.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations for Spotify

### 🛠️ 1. Discovery Baseline
* Introduce an internal structural constraint within personalized frameworks (like Discover Weekly) dedicated to providing a fixed footprint exclusively for emerging talent.

### 📅 2. Automated Artist Planning
* Create an in-app calculator that automates artists' planning and release cadence within the platform's infrastructure using the artist’s historical data and listener base profiles to optimize release timing.
* Provide insights on metrics such as streams, playlist adds, skip rate, completion rate, drop-off points, and listener conversion.
* Inform the exact holiday/season for emerging talent to avoid releasing tracks simultaneously with major record labels.

### 🎛️ 3. Recommendation System Optimization (RSO)
* Provide transparency for artists through an internal tool showing how close they are to triggering algorithm pushes.
* Create an in-app dashboard that visually shows where an artist's tracks sit relative to algorithmic benchmarks (e.g., *"Your track is at 14% save rate; you need X more saves to be pushed into Discover Weekly"*).

---

## 📈 Data Stack & Methodology
* **Data Sources:** 16 years of historical Spotify data (2009 to 2025) downloaded from Kaggle.
* **Core Metrics Evaluated:** Popularity ranking for tracks (singles vs. albums), artist rankings, artist names, and more.
* **Probability of "Making it" to Mainstream:** Monte Carlo Simulation (100,000 simulations) that takes artists and their track ranking data.
